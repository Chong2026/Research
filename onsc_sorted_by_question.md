# ONSC / Div Ct cases sorted by question (from onsc_snowball.py, 23 Oct 2026)

Method: ONSC citations found inside SCC/ONCA decisions matching each question.
Removed: the decision under appeal ("On appeal from ... reported at"), and citations
whose surrounding text is off-topic. Propositions below are taken from the citing
ONCA/SCC passage only. Read the ONSC decision itself before relying on it.

## Q1 - Enforcing an unpaid costs order
- 20 Toronto Street Holdings Ltd. v. Coffee, Tea or Me Bakeries Inc., 2001 CanLII 28048 (ON SC), 53 O.R. (3d) 360, para 5 - r. 60.08(16): court may make any order it deems just on a garnishment motion. [cited in 2023 ONCA 773]
- Bank of Canada v. Ehtisham, 2010 ONSC 1528, para 9 - Rule 60 writs (seizure and sale, sequestration, possession) are tools to enforce a pre-existing order. [2022 ONCA 89]
- Transportaction Lease Systems Inc. v. Spire Freezers Ltd., 2011 ONSC 5509 - a debt not yet due and payable is not subject to garnishment. [2020 ONCA 530]
- Michaud v. Coreslab Structures (Ont.) Inc., 2012 ONSC 355 - a writ of execution does not bind land the debtor holds in trust. [2020 ONCA 250]
- Seed to read (ONCA): Rathod v. Chijindu, 2024 ONCA 625 - payment of overdue costs ordered before the party is heard further; r. 57.03(1) 30-day default.

## Q2 - Interim stay of the costs order / post-judgment interest
- 1247902 Ontario Inc. v. Carlisle Power Systems Ltd., [2003] O.J. No. 6300 (Div. Ct.), para 10, aff'd [2005] O.J. No. 118 (C.A.) - the power to stay a "proceeding" extends to post-judgment execution. [2025 ONCA 446, K.K. v. M.M.]
- Kitmitto v. Ontario Securities Commission, 2023 ONSC 1739 (Div. Ct.), para 19 - risk of financial harm, even bankruptcy, is not enough for irreparable harm. [2025 ONCA 465, OSC v. Cacoeli]
- Caja Paraguaya ... v. Obregon, 2022 ONSC 2360, para 11 - ongoing defiance of orders weighs against a stay on the balance of convenience. [2022 ONCA 724]
- Post-judgment interest while stayed: nothing on point surfaced. Search CanLII/Westlaw directly: "s. 129" + "stay" + "costs".

## Q3 - r. 59.06(2)(a) reconsideration; Sagaz note-up; certification
### Test and factors
- Wesbell Networks Inc. (Receiver of) v. Bell Canada, 2013 ONSC 7814, and Irving Shipbuilding Inc. v. Schmidt, 2014 ONSC 5089, para 57 - cited in Mehedi, 2015 ONCA 670 as examples of courts wrongly reducing Sagaz to two questions; Mehedi holds the test is broader (finality, cogency, delay, fairness, prejudice).
- Saggi v. Grilone, 2021 ONSC 2276 - mover must show the fraud/new fact was unknown at the hearing and could not have been raised with due diligence. [2023 ONCA 74]
- Trainor v. Canada, 2012 ONSC 3450 (Div. Ct.), para 5 - a change in the jurisprudence is not a "new fact" under r. 59.06. [Mujagic v. Kamps, 2015 ONCA 360] (relevant to "pronouncements in law")
- Brown v. Municipal Property Assessment Corp., 2014 ONSC 7137, paras 25-27 - cited with Sagaz paras 59-61 against reopening that would extend a limitation period. [SS&C Technologies v. BNY Mellon, 2024 ONCA 675]
- Book v. Cociardi, 2022 ONSC 3125, aff'd 2024 ONCA 589 - the "Book factors" for setting aside an order ending an action. [Ratnasingam v. Balasubramaniam, 2025 ONCA 898]
- Fatahi-Ghandehari v. Wilson, 2022 ONSC 4799 (LeMay J.) - r. 59.06 motion dismissed because the allegations had already been raised and rejected. [2022 ONCA 858; 2023 ONCA 74]
- Salehi v. APEO, 2015 ONSC 7271 (Diamond J.) - the underlying dismissal in the Salehi saga; see 2022 ONCA 511 and 2025 ONCA 562 (59.06 motion after appeal belongs in the Superior Court).
### Class action / certification-specific
- Turner v. York University, 2011 ONSC 6151, paras 6, 60-65 - a certification finding that the pleading discloses no cause of action can give rise to res judicata barring a later attempt to amend. [David v. Loblaw, 2025 ONCA 830; 2016 ONCA 250] ** key **
- David v. Loblaw, 2024 ONSC 5818 (Morgan J.) - decision under appeal in 2025 ONCA 830 (post-certification amendment / res judicata). Read with the ONCA reasons.
- Jeffery v. London Life Insurance Co., 2017 ONSC 4480 ("R. 59.06 Reasons") - r. 59.06 motion in a class action; appeal at 2018 ONCA 716.
- Trustees of the Millwright Regional Council of Ontario Pension Trust Fund v. Celestica Inc., 2013 ONSC 1502, paras 30, 32 (Perell J.) - scope of r. 59.06(1) (slips/clerical errors only). [2025 ONCA 326]
- Fontaine v. Canada (AG), 2018 ONSC 103, paras 51, 170 - limits on reopening settled IAP claims. [J.W. v. Canada, 2019 SCC 20] (marginal; finality in class settlement context)

## Q4 - Rule 2.1 in response to a reconsideration motion
- Closest match (ONCA): Wilson v. Fatahi-Ghandehari, 2023 ONCA 74 - r. 2.1.01 request to summarily dismiss an appeal from the dismissal of a r. 59.06 motion; the moving party relied on fraud already raised earlier and had over $150,000 in unpaid costs. The reasoning points to dismissal; confirm the outcome. Below: 2022 ONSC 4799.
- Gao v. Ontario WSIB, 2014 ONSC 6100 and 2014 ONSC 6497, para 15 - leading statements of the r. 2.1 test and hallmarks of frivolous/vexatious proceedings (cited 10x by ONCA).
- Markowa v. Adamson Cosmetic Facial Surgery Inc., 2014 ONSC 6664, para 3 - purpose of r. 2.1; abuse must be apparent on the face of the pleadings. [2023 ONCA 140]
- Husain v. Craig, 2015 ONSC 1754, para 10 - caution: "what if the plaintiff's allegations are true?" [2017 ONCA 625] (a point the plaintiff here will rely on)
- Evans v. Snieg, 2019 ONSC 7270, para 30 - a prior r. 37.16 order "should not be lightly disregarded". [M.E. v. CAS Toronto, 2025 ONCA 838]
- Tran v. OIPRD, 2023 ONSC 3207 (Div. Ct.) - r. 2.1.01 dismissal is a final disposition. [2024 ONCA 264]
- Joshi v. Ontario, 2019 ONSC 2444 - control of the court's process is not displaced by a constitutional claim. [2026 ONCA 444] (useful because the plaintiff pleads Charter breaches)
- 2019 ONSC 2637 - vexatious litigant whose primary objective was to relitigate issues decided by final orders. [2019 ONCA 717]
- No class action example of r. 2.1.02 against a reconsideration motion surfaced; that will need a direct ONSC search.

## Q5 - Security for costs (r. 56.01(1)(c) unpaid costs)
- No ONSC case on r. 56.01(1)(c) specifically surfaced in the SCC/ONCA text. Search ONSC directly; Endean v. St. Joseph's General Hospital, 2021 ONSC 2523 (check citation) is the lead from commentary.
- Trillium Motor World Ltd. v. General Motors of Canada Ltd., 2016 ONCA 702 (seed) citing United General Contracting Ltd. v. Sioux Lookout, 2012 ONSC 542 - impecuniosity and security in a class action, including class members' ability to contribute. ** key **
- Impecuniosity evidence: T.S. Publishing Group Inc. v. Shokar, 2013 ONSC 1755 (Master) ("robust particularity"); Marvello Construction v. Santos, 2017 ONSC 3913 (Master), para 10 (full financial disclosure). [2017 ONCA 741; 2017 ONCA 968]
- Stojanovic v. Bulut, 2011 ONSC 874 (Master), para 5 - broad discretion; order must not prevent a bona fide claim from proceeding. [2017 ONCA 741]
- Baker v. Rego, 2013 ONSC 3309 - a party with assets can post security, e.g. a letter of credit. [2016 ONCA 833; 2016 ONCA 89; 2017 ONCA 651]
- General Products Inc. v. Actiwin Co., 2015 ONSC 6923, para 18 - higher onus on a corporate plaintiff to show sufficient assets. [2019 ONCA 164]
